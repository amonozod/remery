What is Reclaiming Memory?

A project dedicated to exploring the history of Uzbekistan beyond textbooks and commonly told narratives.

Our goal is not simply to learn about the past, but to feel it, question it, and look at it from new perspectives. Through research, real voices, and overlooked narratives, we aim to reconnect with a past that continues to shape who we are today.

And this is only the beginning. New formats, perspectives, and interactive experiences will be introduced throughout the project to make history more engaging, personal, and meaningful.

What to expect

  1. Introduction to Remery

    Discover what Reclaiming Memory is, why it was created, and what we hope to achieve.

  2. Conversations with historians

    Hear from historians and explore different perspectives on Uzbekistan's past.

  3. Special guest talk

    A special guest will share their knowledge, experience, and perspective on history and memory.

  4. Networking

    Meet other young people interested in history, culture, research, and Uzbekistan's heritage.

  5. Join the Remery community

    Become part of a community that wants to explore, question, and preserve the stories that shape us.

  6. Closing ceremony

    Wrap up the event together and look ahead to what comes next for Reclaiming Memory.

History is not only what is written in books.

It is also what is remembered, what is forgotten, what is questioned, and what is passed from one generation to another. Come explore it with us.
